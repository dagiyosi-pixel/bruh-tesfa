# BRUH TESFA (ብሩህ ተስፋ) — 30-Day Practical Launch Playbook
**Objective**: Recruit the first cohort of 25 vetted Ethiopian tutors, secure 15 genuine parent requests, and personally execute 8 paying placements in Addis Ababa.

---

## Week 1: Foundational Tutor Cohort (Days 1–7)
- [ ] **University Campus Outreach**:
  - Focus: Addis Ababa University (AAU 4 Kilo Faculty of Natural Sciences, AAU 5 Kilo Institute of Technology) and ASTU.
  - Post recruitment flyers in university noticeboards: *"Teach High School STEM & Earn 400–600 ETB/hr in Your Neighborhood"*.
  - Share link to `/become-a-tutor?utm_source=campus_outreach`.
- [ ] **Telegram Community Setup**:
  - Establish the official `@bruh_tesfa_tutoring_ethiopia` and `@bruhtesfa_announcements`.
  - Share free educational study guides (e.g., Grade 8 Regional Exam Review, EUEE Physics/Math problem walk-throughs).
- [ ] **Initial Vetting Batch**:
  - Conduct 15-minute phone interviews with the first 30 applicants.
  - Verify Fayda National IDs and AAU/ASTU degree certificates.
  - In Admin Dashboard (`/admin`), approve the top 20 candidates. This automatically publishes them to `/tutors`.

---

## Week 2: Parent Acquisition in Prime Sub-Cities (Days 8–14)
- [ ] **Targeted Local Presence**:
  - Focus on high-density residential family neighborhoods: Bole (Atlas, Medhanialem), CMC, Sarbet, Lebu, and Summit.
  - Share localized tutor cards in community school parent groups and diaspora family circles.
- [ ] **Educational Content Campaign**:
  - Publish articles explaining the SQ3R study method for Ethiopian National Curriculum exams.
  - Emphasize the **14-day replacement safeguard** and **0 ETB parent registration fee**.
- [ ] **Initial Request Inflow**:
  - Monitor incoming requests on `/admin`. Aim for 8–10 parent requests.

---

## Week 3: Candidate Matching & Introductory Lessons (Days 15–21)
- [ ] **Algorithmic Shortlisting**:
  - Use the Admin Matcher (`/admin` -> Requests -> "Find Candidates") to calculate compatibility scores based on subject, grade, sub-city proximity, and budget.
  - Propose 2 top candidate profiles to each parent via phone/Telegram.
- [ ] **Scheduling & Parental Presence Briefing**:
  - Confirm the introductory lesson time with both parties.
  - Reiterate safeguarding rule: Parent or adult guardian must be present in the residence during all in-person lessons.
- [ ] **Follow-up Call**:
  - Call the parent 2 hours after the first lesson: *"Did the tutor arrive on time? Was the student engaged?"*

---

## Week 4: Placement Confirmation & 60% Fee Reconciliation (Days 22–30)
- [ ] **Placement Agreement Generation**:
  - In `/admin`, create the official placement contract locking in the 60% placement commission on the first month value.
- [ ] **Payment Reconciliation**:
  - Collect the placement fee via Telebirr merchant or CBE Birr.
  - Log the transaction reference in `/admin` (Placements -> Reconcile).
- [ ] **Tutor Independence Handover**:
  - Reassure the tutor: Months 2 onwards carry **0% commission**. The family pays 100% directly to them.
- [ ] **Referral Loop**:
  - Request a real parent review after lesson 4 to feature on the website with permission.
